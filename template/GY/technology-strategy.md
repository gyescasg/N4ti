## Recommendation

Pending orchestrator analysis. Recommend technologies using product fit,
simplicity, maintainability, deployment, ecosystem, team familiarity,
portability, migration risk, and lock-in.

## Alternatives

Record at least one serious alternative for high-impact choices.

## Review Triggers

- A new platform or incompatible requirement appears.
- Security, scale, support, or operating cost changes materially.
- A critical dependency becomes unsupported.

Technology changes require an active decision record. GY must not continuously
re-propose migrations without a trigger.
